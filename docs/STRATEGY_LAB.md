# Strategy Lab V0.92–V0.93 (experimental)

- A separate **Strategy Lab tab** in the existing app. No new app, repo, backend, or paid API.
- Six editable TWSE watch zones. The historic thresholds are research markers, **not valuations or trading orders**.
- State is part of the existing PortfolioPilot AppState, durable iOS storage and JSON backup; legacy backups without the optional field remain parseable.
- Original Official Scanner, portfolio risk view, and alerts are retained, not replaced.
- Technical calculation: 14-period Wilder RSI; 20-period SMA and EMA; 12/26/9 MACD; Taiwan smoothed KD-K (9-period RSV with initial K=50); today's volume / **preceding** 20 trading-day average volume.
- For the six initially listed TWSE securities, a no-cost GitHub Actions workflow fetches monthly TWSE STOCK_DAY OHLCV on a delayed schedule. The cache contains only verified downloaded rows; reported failed months are preserved and **block signal/backtest eligibility**, rather than silently filling gaps. 
- Daily close-only cache can support RSI/MACD/SMA/EMA but cannot support KD or volume filters. Missing OHLCV produces **insufficient data** and no Strategy B trigger.
- Data and sources are dated. TWSE OHLCV is unadjusted for dividends and splits. Avoid treating an old signal as current. No intraday streaming, broker login, automatic orders, real-time push or proven strategy win rate.
- **V0.93 / experimental:** cost-aware, long-only historical backtesting. Entry signals are evaluated at each close; simulated entries execute at the *next* session's recorded open. Same-bar stop/target ambiguity uses the adverse stop first, and gap-down exits use the real next open. Default assumptions: 14.25 bps one-way fee, 5 bps each-way slippage and 30 bps sell-side transaction tax; all adjustable. Report total/annualized returns, drawdown, trade count, win rate, mean trade P&L, Profit Factor, after-cost buy-and-hold baseline, chronological fixed-parameter in/out-of-sample summaries (70%/30%) and descriptive bull/bear/sideways breakdown. **This is not yet corporate-action-adjusted, not cross-validated and not execution-grade.** Samples with incomplete OHLCV are blocked; no transaction means no valid win-rate inference. Real-world trading fees and filled prices may differ.
- **V0.94–V0.95 / upcoming:** portfolio-relative position sizing, ETF exposure and cash guardrails; app-open and after-close notification paths with clear limits. iOS TestFlight signing remains a separate release gate.
- For manual OHLCV refresh: run `node --test scripts/lib/strategy-ohlcv.check.mjs` and `node scripts/update-strategy-ohlcv.mjs`, only after reviewing the public TWSE source and accepting its usage policies.
